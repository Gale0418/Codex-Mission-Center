# Creative Cross-Domain Council

This is the early North Star and architecture-divergence council. For a later-stage research, implementation, optimization, or decision revisit, classify complexity and use the [Dynamic Expert Council Gate](dynamic-expert-council.md) instead.

## When to Use

Run this council when the mission is open-ended and benefits from invention, differentiation, experience design, system design, architecture, or a way out of a local optimum.

Skip it for deterministic commands, formatting, mechanical synchronization, routine maintenance, and small fixes with one established solution.

## Extract the Mechanism

Describe the underlying forces or tensions before choosing experts. Examples include airflow, heat, noise, grip, spatial flow, information flow, feedback delay, trust, scarcity, coordination, and recovery.

Select a small set of fields that solve analogous mechanisms. Do not use a fixed corporate department list. For a hair-dryer problem, useful transfers might come from thermal engineering, industrial design, architectural airflow, wind machinery, acoustics, or biomimicry.

## Diverge

Generate candidates through analogy, inversion, combination, scale transfer, constraint removal, and transfer from apparently unrelated fields. Do not reject ideas early because they are unfamiliar or initially expensive.

Each candidate should state:

- source field
- transferred principle
- value created by the combination
- smallest useful experiment

## Converge

Evaluate candidates for user value, engineering feasibility, cost, safety, licensing, maintainability, and testability. Preserve at least one unexpected but feasible candidate instead of selecting only the most conventional idea.

## Presentation

Use a hybrid summary:

- Normally show key insights, blind spots, candidates, and the recommendation.
- Expand individual professional viewpoints only when a material disagreement changes the decision.
- End with one question when mission-level uncertainty remains.

The council must change a question, option, or task design. Do not output decorative role-play dialogue.
