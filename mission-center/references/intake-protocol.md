# Intake Protocol

## Goal

Turn a vague request into a clear project statement before creating or changing the workspace.

## Question Order

Ask one question at a time, in this order:

1. What is the goal?
2. What does success look like?
3. What are the hard constraints?
4. What is the deadline or urgency?
5. Who owns the outcome?
6. What is the first milestone?
7. What is the main risk?
8. What should definitely not happen?

## Stop Criteria

Stop asking questions when all of these are clear:

- the project goal
- the first deliverable
- the success criteria
- the constraints
- the owner
- the main risk

If the user cannot answer one item, record it as an open question and continue with the next most important one.

## Intake Output

Write the result into `project.md` and `progress.md` before starting execution.
